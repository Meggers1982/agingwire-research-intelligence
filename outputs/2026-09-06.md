**Why it matters:** Facility-based care is competing for workers against a home-based sector growing more than twice as fast and paying more: average hourly earnings for July 2026 run $28.04 in home health against $25.65 in assisted living and CCRCs. The CMS side of this is refreshed reference data rather than a new finding, so the piece rests its argument on the employment and earnings series and uses the files to make it concrete facility by facility.
