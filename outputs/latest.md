## Bigger picture: feature pitch

**The pattern:** On 08/01/26 CMS refreshed essentially the entire facility-level record for nursing homes in one pass: Provider Information, Health Deficiencies, Fire Safety Deficiencies, Penalties, Ownership, MDS Quality Measures, Survey Summary, Inspection Dates, Citation Code Look-up, Nursing Home Data Collection Intervals, Medicare Claims Quality Measures and the Skilled Nursing Facility Quality Reporting Program file. Alongside that, BLS put nursing care facilities employment for August 2026 at 1,590.20 thousands of jobs, up 2.1% year over year, while home health care services employment reached 1,896.40 thousands of jobs, up 5.0%. Justice in Aging published a Medicaid Home and Community-Based Services primer on 08/20/26 addressing the policy gaps that limit access, and the Center for Retirement Research compared long-term care systems in Israel and the U.S. on 09/03/26. The substantive thread is a mismatch of scrutiny: the public record is thickest, most granular and freshest exactly where employment is growing slowest, and thinnest where growth is fastest. A family can look up a single nursing home's citations, fines and owners county by county; the side of long-term care adding jobs at more than twice that rate has no equivalent row-by-row file in this run. Seventeen of the 18 items in this cluster went unmatched by the publishers we monitor, and the newest is a day old.

**Why pitch this now:** State Medicaid financing is moving underneath long-term care right now. KFF's 08/20/26 brief on provider taxes describes how the rules governing them are changing because of the 2025 reconciliation law and the regulations implementing it, and summarizes which changes may affect each state. Provider taxes are what many states lean on to fund nursing facility and HCBS payments, so a fully refreshed penalty, ownership and staffing file arrives in the same weeks that the money behind those facilities is being rewritten. Add HUD's Fair Market Rents notice for FY 2027 published 09/01/26 and the Housing Trust Fund FY 2026 allocation notice published 09/04/26, and the cost side of aging in place is being reset in the same window.

**Angle:** "The Fine Print on 1,590,200 Jobs" — a data-reported piece that pairs the refreshed CMS nursing home files with the BLS employment split, written for families choosing between a facility and home-based care and for the operators and state agencies on the other side of that choice. Build it state by state from the Ownership, Penalties and Health Deficiencies files, then set the facility count against home health's 5.0% employment growth and the HCBS access gaps Justice in Aging describes.
