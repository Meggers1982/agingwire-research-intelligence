## Bigger picture: feature pitch

**The pattern:** On 08/01/26 CMS refreshed essentially the entire public record on nursing homes at once — Provider Information, Ownership, Penalties, Health Deficiencies, Fire Safety Deficiencies, Survey Summary, Inspection Dates, MDS Quality Measures, Medicare Claims Quality Measures, the Skilled Nursing Facility Quality Reporting Program file, Nursing Home Data Collection Intervals and the Citation Code Look-up, with Facility Affiliation Data on 07/31/26, Patient-Reported Outcomes on 08/05/26 and Utilization Data on 08/11/26. What connects them is not that they arrived together but what they now measure against: BLS puts nursing care facilities employment at 1,590.20 thousand jobs in August 2026, up 2.1% year over year, while home health care services employment sits at 1,896.40 thousand, up 5.0%. The staffing that drives the Five-Star Rating System is growing at less than half the rate of the home-based alternative, and Justice in Aging's 08/20/26 primer on Medicaid Home and Community-Based Services documents the policy gaps that limit access to that alternative. The Center for Retirement Research's 09/03/26 comparison of long-term care in Israel and the United States frames the same bind as structural rather than local. So the facility scorecard has a new edition, the workforce behind the ratings is thinning relative to home care, and the escape route from facility care is documented as rationed. This cluster coheres at 0.256 across four independent sources, 17 of its 18 items unmatched by monitored publishers.

**Why pitch this now:** The federal calendar turns on October 1. HUD published FY 2027 Fair Market Rents on 09/01/26 and the Housing Trust Fund FY 2026 allocation notice on 09/04/26, both of which reset what subsidized housing costs and where the money goes for the coming year. Families weighing a nursing home this fall are doing it against a citation record that now covers a fresh three-year window, and against a home care labor market that is pulling workers in the other direction.

**Angle:** "The New Nursing Home Report Card, County by County" — a data-driven consumer guide built from the refreshed CMS provider files, showing how to read penalties, ownership and staffing for a specific facility, paired with the BLS staffing trend that explains why the numbers look the way they do. Written for adult children shortlisting facilities in the next 90 days, with a companion trade read for operators on where the labor is going.
