## Bigger picture: feature pitch

**The pattern:** Medicare and Medicaid is again the run's busiest beat, 38 items from 8 sources, and it is not one story. The thread that is real is narrower and newer: on 11/09/26 the Federal Register carried three CY 2027 Medicare final rules on the same day. The Calendar Year 2027 Home Health Prospective Payment System rate update finalizes a temporary behavior adjustment along with recalibrated case-mix weights, updated functional impairment levels, comorbidity subgroups and low-utilization policy. The CY 2027 Physician Fee Schedule rule resets Part B payment and codifies the Medicare Prescription Drug Inflation Rebate Program. The Medicare Drug Price Negotiation Program rule finalizes and codifies the negotiation program and new Part D policies required by the Inflation Reduction Act of 2022. A fourth document the same day corrects a typographical error in the 10/20/26 proposed rule implementing Sections 6219, 6220 and 6224 of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2026. Three of these are logged as lightly matched by monitored publishers; the correction is a confirmed gap. Read together, they are the price list for 2027 across the home visit, the office visit and the pharmacy counter, set in a single day of filings.

**Why it matters:** Home health is the setting older adults are moving into, 1,903.60 thousands of jobs in October 2026, up 4.4% year over year, and the HH PPS rule changes the case-mix and low-utilization math that decides which of those visits get paid for and at what level. The Part D and negotiation rules decide what a beneficiary hands over at the counter. None of that reaches a Medicare household as news; it reaches them as a 2027 bill or a changed plan of care. KFF's 11/07/26 analysis of the proposed 340B cut shows the same mechanism one level up: a payment change that moves revenue away from safety-net hospitals and toward for-profit hospitals without anyone voting on it.

**Why pitch this now:** The rules were published 11/09/26 and are final, not proposed, so the 2027 numbers they set are the numbers providers will build budgets and schedules against over the next six weeks. The correction to the 10/20/26 proposed rule shows that the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2026 implementation is still mid-stream and not yet locked.

**Angle:** Whether a household on Medicare can find out, from the rules as written, what changes for them in 2027, when the three documents that determine it published on one day and only in the Federal Register.
