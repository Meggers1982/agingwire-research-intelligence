- **Angle:** "The Nursing Home Record, County by County." A consumer-first data piece for adult children and spouses who are two to eight weeks away from a placement decision, with a trade companion for operators on what the ownership and penalty files reveal about their own market.
- **Potential headlines:** What the newly refreshed federal files say about the nursing home near you. Nursing home jobs grew 2.1 percent while home health added 5 percent. Every fine and citation from the past three years is posted again.
