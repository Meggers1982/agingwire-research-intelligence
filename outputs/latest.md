## Bigger picture: feature pitch

**The pattern:** On 08/01/26 CMS refreshed essentially the entire public accountability record for nursing homes in one batch: Provider Information (the file behind the Five-Star Rating System, including certified beds and staffing), Ownership, Penalties, Health Deficiencies, Fire Safety Deficiencies, Survey Summary, Inspection Dates, MDS Quality Measures, Medicare Claims Quality Measures, the Skilled Nursing Facility Quality Reporting Program provider file, and the Nursing Home Data Collection Intervals table that tells you which quarters each score covers. Facility Affiliation Data came 07/31/26, Patient-Reported Outcomes 08/05/26, Utilization Data 08/11/26. What connects them is not the refresh date but what sits underneath: BLS puts nursing care facilities employment at 1,590.20 thousands of jobs in August 2026, up 2.1% year over year, the slowest growth of the three care settings BLS tracks, while home health care services employment reached 1,896.40 thousands of jobs, up 5.0%. So the staffing measures inside the freshly rewritten star ratings are being generated by a workforce barely growing, and the community-based alternative families are steered toward is the subject of a Justice in Aging primer published 08/20/26 on the policy gaps that limit access to Medicaid home and community-based services. The Center for Retirement Research put the same tension in comparative terms on 09/03/26 in its look at long-term care systems in Israel and the United States. One story: the report card got rewritten, the workforce behind the grades did not change much, and the exit ramp is rationed.

**Why pitch this now:** The refreshed CMS files are the version families will be reading through the fall, and the money rules around them are moving at the same time. KFF's brief of 08/20/26 lays out how provider tax rules are changing under the 2025 reconciliation law and the regulations implementing it, state by state. On the housing side HUD published FY 2027 Fair Market Rents on 09/01/26 and the Housing Trust Fund FY 2026 allocation notice on 09/04/26. Anyone comparing a nursing home, an assisted living community and staying home is making that decision in a month when the ratings, the reimbursement rules and the rent baselines all just turned over.

**Angle:** "The August Rewrite" — a localizable investigation and consumer guide built on the 08/01/26 CMS nursing home files, matching each facility's staffing and citation record against the flat national nursing-home employment trend and the HCBS access gaps documented on 08/20/26. Primary audience is families choosing or auditing a facility in the next 90 days; the data is county-level, so it runs in a national version with state cutdowns.
