# Editorial scoring

Evidence and storyability are scored separately.

## Evidence grade
- A: primary government/admin data; strong peer-reviewed evidence; transparent major longitudinal surveys.
- B: reputable academic/nonprofit/industry study with disclosed methods.
- C: commercial or association analysis with limitations.
- D: media-only claim/anecdote/unclear methods; lead only.

## Story score (0-5)
Novelty, human impact, localization, consumer usefulness, B2B relevance, visualization potential, timeliness and original-analysis potential.

Apply penalties for duplication, weak methods, commercial conflicts and unsupported local inference.
