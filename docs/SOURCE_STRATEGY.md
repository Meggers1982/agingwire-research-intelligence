# Source strategy

## Academic
Keep the existing ~146-journal PubMed list as the medical backbone. Add only clear gap-fillers for housing/environment, social care, work/retirement, technology and health-services research.

## Government/data
Prioritize ACS, HRS, NHATS/NSOC, CDC Healthy Aging/BRFSS, CMS provider and beneficiary data, Medicaid/LTSS, HUD/AHS, BLS, SSA, FTC and CFPB.

## Nonprofit/think tank
Use recurring surveys and policy research from AARP PPI, KFF, National Alliance for Caregiving, NCOA, PHI, CRR, EBRI and others. Preserve methodology and label advocacy/commercial interests.

## Industry
NIC and provider/association research are crucial for occupancy, supply, pricing, staffing, development and operations. Company/REIT disclosures are signals and must be labeled commercial.
