# B2B and B2C media layer

The two media universes remain separate.

## B2B
Use trade publications to understand operator priorities, coverage saturation, likely syndication targets and industry framing. Examples in the registry include Senior Housing News, McKnight's, Seniors Housing Business, Skilled Nursing News, Home Health Care News, Generations Today and association media.

## B2C
Use consumer outlets to assess usefulness to older adults/caregivers and identify distribution opportunities for housing, retirement, caregiving, dementia, finance and lifestyle stories.

## Rule
A media item creates a `coverage_signal`, not evidence. Trace claims to the underlying study/dataset/report wherever possible.
